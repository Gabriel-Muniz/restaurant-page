Welcome to Cozinha Palestra

In 1914, Sociedade Esportiva Palmeiras was founded, and with it, our love for cooking! Located on the street of the old Palestra Itália stadium, now Nubank Parque, Cozinha Palestra is a meeting point for Palmeiras fans on game days. Serving the best of what we have, check out our menu!

Since then we've done our best to bring the experience of being a Palmeirense to our clients, serving the dishes the remind us of our passion.
